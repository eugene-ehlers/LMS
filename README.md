# LMS

Post-origination loan management service for South African credit providers. Multi-tenant, white-label. Identity, organisations, memberships, enrolment and mandates are owned by Enigma. This service keeps the account, the balances and the ledger.

The Day-1 financial model is [docs/account-balance-transaction-model.md](docs/account-balance-transaction-model.md). It covers the loan account, the section 101 components, the append-only ledger, lay-by holds, and the posting rules for booking, payments, schedules, top-ups and restructures. Choices the National Credit Act does not settle are numbered and left unsigned until credit-law sign-off.

# LMS

Post-origination loan management service for South African credit providers. Multi-tenant, white-label. Identity, organisations, memberships, enrolment and mandates are owned by Enigma. This service keeps the account, the balances and the ledger.

The Day-1 financial model is [docs/account-balance-transaction-model.md](docs/account-balance-transaction-model.md). Locked decisions cover recognition-date value dates, capitalised and separate initiation fees, zero-rate campaigns, linked lay-bys, day count, daily accrual, client statements, external schedules and payment frequencies. The remaining legal and business gates are listed in that document and block only the behaviour they control.
